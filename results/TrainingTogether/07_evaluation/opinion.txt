Why MA 20 is declining from ~35k (real)
Look at the lifespan chart — there are repeated spikes to 0 appearing in the 20k–30k batch range. This means the autopilot is now triggering and misfiring: when it kicks in, it forces the commander toward q_fire_pos, but q_fire_pos is derived from the scout's local map centroid + altitude-scaled FOV. If the scout is flying at 120m altitude, its FOV is 180m wide and the centroid can be wildly off. The autopilot is steering the commander toward a bad estimate of fire position, which goes negative, which re-triggers autopilot — a feedback loop driving reward down.
